Concept Art & World Building

As anEvony player for nearly 6 years, I have always been driven to innovate within the game. For too long, these high-level strategy and feature concepts remained as mental notes.

Over the last 2 years, I have dedicated myself to professional Design and Multimedia (FAD) training. This journey discovered my passion for creating user-centric UI/UX and visual assets for game environments. Merging my deep gameplay knowledge with this new technical expertise, I finally gained the skills to transform strategic concepts into concrete, professional visual assets and Game Design proposals.

Today, I am proud to present my vision for the strategic and aesthetic evolution of Evony. This page documents my journey of creativity, development, and official submission of ideas to the development team.
